Misa Hayase

She is a character in The Super Dimension Fortress Macross television series and The Super Dimension Fortress Macross: Do You Remember Love? film. A First Lieutenant in the U.N. Spacy, she served as the Tactical Operations Officer on the SDF-1 Macross bridge during Space War I. Responsible for tactical operations and mission coordination, she is known for her disciplined and serious demeanor.
