Lynn Minmay

In the beginning, she was simply a Chinese restaurant waitress in high school with dreams of being a singer. Since Space War I, her exploits and her role in the Battle with the Boddole Zer Main Fleet have become the stuff of legends and countless retellings. She performed a Sayonara Concert Tour before embarking upon the Megaroad-01 on a colonization voyage in 2012. She was last heard just before disappearing with the Megaroad-01 in 2016.
