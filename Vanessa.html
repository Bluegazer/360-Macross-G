Vanessa Laird

She is a character in The Super Dimension Fortress Macross television series and The Super Dimension Fortress Macross: Do You Remember Love? film. A First Lieutenant in the U.N. Spacy, she served as a Macross Operator on the SDF-1 Macross bridge during Space War I. As one of the bridge operators, she is primarily responsible for external communications and information analysis.
