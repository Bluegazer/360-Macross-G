Shammy Milliome

She is a character in The Super Dimension Fortress Macross television series and The Super Dimension Fortress Macross: Do You Remember Love? film. Shammy is the youngest among the main bridge crews, and the most precocious. She has long hair that reaches her waist and possesses a little body. She has a sensitive nature that often goes beyond the realm of common sense.
