Kim Kabirov

Kim is a U.N. Spacy officer serving as a Macross Operator on the SDF-1 Macross bridge during Space War I. She is mainly in charge of ship's control and broadcasting system. Has a short hair cut and boyish appearance and is known for being a little forward towards others. She is a sloppy in her work, as she tends to gossip, hoping nobody would catch her.
