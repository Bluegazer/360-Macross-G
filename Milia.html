Milia Fallyna Jenius

She is renown as the "Zentradi ace Milia" even among her male counterparts, and respected for her prowess in her powered suit. After an unusual courtship, she marry with ace pilot Max Jenius, and have seven daughters. After Space War I, she led the Dancing Skull special forces team with Max. She also helmed the Eagle Nest Aerial Tactics Center as "Eagle Mother" before being elected mayor of Macross 7's City 7.
