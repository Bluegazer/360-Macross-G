Claudia LaSalle

She is a character in The Super Dimension Fortress Macross television series and The Super Dimension Fortress Macross: Do You Remember Love? film. A Major in the U.N. Spacy, she served as the Weapons Systems Officer on the SDF-1 Macross bridge during Space War I. Responsible for weapons control and combat operations, she is known for her strong, professional demeanor and close friendship with Misa Hayase.
